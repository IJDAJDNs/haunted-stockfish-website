Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Haunted Stockfish collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Purchase details: your email address and the plan you bought. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • License data: your license key and your PC’s hardware ID (a code made from your computer, used to lock the key to that PC), plus the times a key was created and activated.
  • Messages you send us: support emails and anything you submit in the feedback form on our site.

How we use it

  • To create, deliver, and check your license key.
  • To answer support requests and move your license to a new PC.
  • To prevent key sharing and fraud, and to process refunds.

Who else sees it

Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy. Our website and license server run on Vercel, with license records stored in a hosted database. Our feedback form is handled by Formspree. We don’t sell your personal information.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your information deleted, email hauntedstockfish@gmail.com. If we delete your license record, your key will stop working.

How long we keep it

We keep license and purchase records for as long as your license is valid, and afterward for as long as needed for refunds, disputes, and tax records.
