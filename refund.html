Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Haunted Stockfish licenses are digital products delivered as a license key. Every purchase is a one-time payment with no automatic renewal.

All sales are final. We don’t give refunds if you change your mind, no longer want to use the software, stop playing, or bought it by mistake. This applies whether or not you have activated your key.

The only cases where we refund

  • The app won’t run on your PC, and our support team can’t fix it within 7 days of you contacting us.
  • You were charged twice, or charged an incorrect amount.
  • We are unable to deliver a working license key to you.

How to ask

Email hauntedstockfish@gmail.com from the address you paid with. Include your license key (it starts with HS-) and a short description of what went wrong. We reply within 24 hours.

After a refund

We turn off the license key. Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Disputes

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. We fix most problems quickly.

Cancellation

There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. Each pass simply ends when its time runs out; the lifetime plan does not expire.

By purchasing, you agree to this policy.
